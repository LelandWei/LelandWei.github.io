<section class="content-section" id="about">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">About</p>
    <h2>Researching secure wireless intelligence at the network edge.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="section-body">
    <p>
      I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Miami. My research sits at the intersection of wireless communications, distributed machine learning, privacy, and protocol optimization.
    </p>
    <p>
      I am particularly interested in making edge learning systems more resilient, spectrum-efficient, energy-efficient, and trustworthy under practical wireless constraints.

<section class="content-section" id="research">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Research Interests</p>
    <h2>Wireless systems, secure distributed learning, and AI-driven protocols.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="research-grid">
    <article>
      <h3>Wireless Security and Next-Generation Networks</h3>
      <p>Security, reliability, and protocol design for Wi-Fi 8, UHR, and future wireless systems.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>Privacy-Preserving Distributed Learning</h3>
      <p>Federated learning, split learning, differential privacy, and secure edge intelligence.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>AI for Wireless Communications</h3>
      <p>Learning-based wireless optimization, resource allocation, and adaptive communication systems.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>Cross-Layer Protocol Optimization</h3>
      <p>Joint PHY/MAC design for channel access, channel sounding, beamforming, and multi-AP coordination.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>Multi-Agent RL and Semantic Communications</h3>
      <p>Collaborative reinforcement learning and semantic-aware transmission for dynamic wireless environments.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
